# The 13-KPI product filter

Source: "Filtering Products: Stage 2" slide from a practitioner video,
supplied by the owner 2026-10-12.

**Rule: a product must satisfy at least 7 of 13 to be shortlisted.**

| # | KPI | What counts as a pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Small in size** | Fits in a shoebox; no dimension over ~35cm |
| 2 | **Easy to ship** | Under ~700g, unbreakable, no liquid, no battery shipping issue |
| 3 | **High margin** | Target CPP from `tools/unit_economics.py` ≥ Rs 280 |
| 4 | **Proof of concept from past winners** | Someone is running ads on it, ideally 6+ months (`RESEARCH-METHOD.md` §2) |
| 5 | **Improves confidence** | Buyer looks or feels better in front of others |
| 6 | **Improves convenience** | Removes a fiddly step from a daily routine |
| 7 | **Saves people time** | A measurable minutes-saved claim is possible |
| 8 | **Solves a real problem / fills a gap** | Statable in the buyer's own words, not "it's pretty" |
| 9 | **Saves people money** | Replaces a recurring cost or a pricier alternative |
| 10 | **Extremely unique** | Not findable on Meesho/Amazon in the same form; personalisation counts |
| 11 | **Improves quality of life** | The daily experience is better, not just the task |
| 12 | **High perceived value** | Looks worth more than we charge — materials, finish, set size |
| 13 | **Woman-dominated audience** | Our buyer is women 25-45, so this is near-mandatory for us |

## How to use it

Score every candidate honestly, including the ones you like. Record the
score **and which KPIs failed** — the failures are what the creative has to
overcome, or what kills it.

A product at 7/13 with #3 (high margin) or #4 (proof of concept) failing is
weaker than its score suggests. **Treat 3 and 4 as near-mandatory**: #3
because the economics decide survival, #4 because it is the only external
evidence that anyone makes money on it.

#10 (extremely unique) is the one that answers the marketplace undercut
problem in `research/FINDING-niche-fails-undercut.md`. A product that
passes 10 cannot be price-compared, so the conversion tax disappears.
