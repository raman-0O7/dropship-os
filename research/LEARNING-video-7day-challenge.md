# Learning notes — 7-day Indian dropshipping challenge (Hindi)

**Source:** `youtube.com/watch?v=miBDRJuQ6Cs` · read via transcript.
The YouTube page title renders in English but the content is Hindi and
India-specific. **This is the video the owner's 13-KPI slide came from.**

Claimed headline: Rs 38,000 revenue in 7 days; Rs 30,000 of sales on
Rs 1,942 ad spend. Challenge started 21 October.

## The 13 KPIs, confirmed from the audio

He reads the list out and it matches the slide. Full version with pass
criteria is now in `KPI-SCORECARD.md`. Rule: **at least 7 of 13.**

His own explanations of the two that carry real weight:

**High margin** — "a Rs 10 item I can sell at Rs 30. 3x. At minimum 2x —
a Rs 300 item sold at Rs 600. But if it is available for Rs 300 and the
market value is Rs 400, there is no point."

That last clause is the marketplace undercut test stated as a margin rule.
Our Rs 1,099 floor and 3.2x markup in `tools/score_products.py` sit right
on his recommendation.

**Proof of concept from past winners** — "we just saw on Ad Library that
it is already selling in the market." Same as `RESEARCH-METHOD.md` §2.

## The number that matters most

> "My net profit comes to around **15%**. And right now I am only testing.
> I have not even started scaling."

**15% net while testing.** That is the third independent source giving
15-20% as the realistic margin, so treat it as the planning number rather
than an optimistic case.

## The discipline worth copying

He does not claim profit from revenue. He builds an estimate sheet with:

- actual ad spend
- actual revenue
- **the RTO rate his supplier predicts**
- → estimated profit

and then says he will wait for all 50 orders to deliver before replacing
estimates with actuals.

> "I will not scale. I will wait for all 50 orders to deliver."

This is exactly the delivered-margin rule in `PLAN.md` §3, from someone
running real money. It is also the discipline the other case-study video
lacked — that one claimed Rs 4,000 profit and admitted mid-video it had
no delivery data yet.

**Build this estimate sheet before the first campaign**, not after. It is
Priority 2 in `AUTOMATION.md`.
