### The drawer-liner contradiction

Shiprocket reported **8,589 orders** for waterproof drawer liner
(`stage1-preliminary.md`), and it was our strongest lead. Google Trends
shows essentially **no search interest** for it in India.

Both can be true: Trends measures *search* demand, while those orders are
marketplace browsing and impulse. For paid social that is not
disqualifying — Reels is discovery, not search — but it does mean no
organic search support, and it makes the Shiprocket figure look like one
large seller's volume rather than a market. **Demoted from first lead to
speculative.**

## Google Trends, India, past 5 years — fad test PASSED

All four terms rise slowly from ~8-12 (Oct 2021) to ~18-25 (Oct 2026). No
peak-and-decline on any of them. Per `RESEARCH-METHOD.md` Stage 1, "flat or
gently rising is healthy" — this is the healthiest shape available, slow
structural growth rather than a fad.

One anomaly: a sharp spike around **Feb 2026**, where makeup organizer hit
100 and drawer organizer ~55. A one-off event, not a trend. Ignore it, but
worth knowing it exists so it is not mistaken for seasonality later.

## Where this leaves us

The category is in slow structural growth and is not a fad — that is the
single most important thing confirmed today. The sub-theme with the most
momentum is **vanity / makeup organisation**, which targets our buyer more
directly than generic home decor and films better than storage boxes.

Revised lead order:

1. **Acrylic makeup / vanity organiser** — Breakout, targets women directly,
   strong visual transformation. Check acrylic's durability over two courier
   legs before anything else; `PRODUCT-SELECTION.md` excludes brittle
   materials and acrylic can crack.
2. **Drawer organiser / divider sets** — highest steady search, bundles to
   the price floor naturally.
3. **Wardrobe / closet organiser** — flat but reliable; "closet organizers"
   rising +550%.
4. **Under-bed storage with wheels** — Breakout, but re-check bulk.
5. ~~Waterproof drawer liner~~ — demoted, no search demand.
